A LITTLE STORY ABOUT US

More Than Just a Cup of Coffee

amerta coffe is a new coffee shop created for people who love good coffee and a cozy atmosphere. We want to make a comfortable place where everyone can relax, meet friends, study, or simply enjoy their favorite cup of coffee.

Our Beginning

  • Starting with a simple love for coffee.
  • Creating a cozy space for everyone.
  • Serving coffee with care and quality.
  • Growing together with our customers.

What We Believe

  • Using quality coffee beans.
  • Supporting local coffee farmers.
  • Reducing plastic waste.
  • Creating a comfortable place for everyone.
